🔔 Enabling Notifications

Follow these steps to receive notifications for reminders and deadlines:

  1. Step 1: In-App Button
    Click the 🔔 Notifs button at the top right of the app. Tap "Allow" when prompted by your browser/phone.
  2. 📱 For iPhone (iOS):
    Notifications on iOS only work if you add the app to your Home Screen! In Safari, tap the 'Share' icon at the bottom ➔ Choose 'Add to Home Screen'. Launch the app from your home screen and enable notifications.
  3. 🤖 For Android / Chrome:
    If you accidentally tapped 'Block': Tap the lock/settings icon next to the URL bar ➔ Select Site Settings ➔ Change Notifications to Allow.
